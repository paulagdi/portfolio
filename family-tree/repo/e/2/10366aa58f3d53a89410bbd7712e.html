Note

Portale Antenati (also known as the "Ancestors Portal") is a public digital archival repository focused on genealogical and historical documentation. Specifically, it is:

A digital archive of scanned civil status registers (birth, marriage, and death records), military draft lists, and university matriculation rolls preserved in the Italian State Archives.
Operated by the Directorate General for Archives within Italy's Ministry of Culture (Ministero della Cultura), in partnership with FamilySearch (the Genealogical Society of Utah).
Born from an agreement signed in June 2011 to digitize and publish online the vast documentary heritage of personal and genealogical interest held by the Italian State Archives.
Covers records dating as early as 1809 through the 1930s, with new records continually being added.
So in terms of repository type, it's best described as a government-run, publicly accessible digital genealogical archive — not a code repository like GitHub, but rather a curated online repository of historical documents aimed at enabling genealogical, historical, demographic, and social science research.
