About me

I’m a Product Designer with 15+ years of design experience and the last 6 years focused on designing complex, specialized tools in Vision AI and HealthTech for B2B SaaS products.

I work end-to-end across research, strategy, interaction design, testing, and implementation to connect business goals with user needs.

I love solving hard problems, simplifying complexity, and creating tools that help people do their jobs better. I’m happiest when I’m learning, designing, and improving, whether that’s a workflow, a product, or a process.

What I focus on

UX/UI Design

Flow mapping, designing complex user journeys, and creating intuitive and accessible experiences.

User Research & Insights

Mixed methods to gain insights from user interviews, behavioral tracking, usability testing and desk research.

Design Strategy & Process

Contributing to product roadmaps by aligning design initiatives with OKRs and business goals, optimizing design processes, and establishing design systems and principles.

Collaboration & Mentorship

Collaborating with PMs, tech, business intelligence, and customer support teams. Guiding junior designers and promoting design excellence.
